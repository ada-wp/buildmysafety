# My list, elephant and progress

October 12, 2026. Ada rejected the earlier path/scene proposal and its replacement elephant. Her confirmed direction is a simple Finch-like home experience: My list, the original elephant, a category progress bar, small completion feedback, and milestones at **1, 3, 6, 9 and 10** completed tasks. This supersedes earlier 1/5/15 proposals. The new clickable design is for review; no live app, database or reward thresholds were changed.

**October 13 addition:** Ada requested a **My progress** tab showing where she is in each category. The navigation is now **My list · Explore · My progress** in the design preview. This supersedes the earlier two-tab design; My list remains the everyday opening screen. The new preview initially opens My progress for review only.

## Five views to review

1. **My list — the everyday opening screen.** Original elephant above one progress bar; saved tasks below with a completion checkbox and a way to inspect their steps. The bar counts distinct completed tasks in the active category toward the first ten. It is not a safety score or a percentage of the thirty-task pool.
2. **Task completed — a brief state on My list.** Completing from the list or task detail advances the same bar. The elephant makes a tiny warm movement, with short acknowledgement and Undo. No full-screen interruption for ordinary completions.
3. **Milestone — one reusable bottom sheet with two moments.** October 13 update: first show the elephant speaking in a short bubble with a gentle hug animation. Tap the elephant to replay the hug, choose “A little check-in” to continue, or return to My list. The optional second moment asks: “How prepared do you feel for a sick day on your own?” Answers: “Still unsure,” “Somewhat prepared,” and “Prepared enough for now.” Not now and Close return to the list. Reuse this at 1, 3, 6, 9 and 10; do not interrupt ordinary task completion with a conversation.
4. **Ready to explore — an optional bottom sheet.** After “Prepared enough for now,” offer “Explore another area” or “Keep building here.” This is her own reported feeling, not app-certified safety or category mastery. She can move on before ten, stay beyond ten, and return with her earlier progress intact.
5. **My progress — a simple overview across categories.** One tappable card per available category shows completed steps, progress toward the first ten, the next milestone and any pending tasks. Show “Your last check-in” only when she has answered; never infer her feeling from the count. A started category returns to its My list; an unstarted category opens its suggestions. Progress and self-reported feelings stay separate for every category. The two existing preview categories demonstrate this pattern without deciding the launch taxonomy.

Keep the established category → concerns/manual request → fitting suggestions → inspect → + save flow. The thirty tasks are options, not required assignments. An empty My list offers a next-step action; it never traps a new user. Category switching retains each category's tasks and count. Existing task-detail and Explore views are reused, not new roadmap pages.

**October 13 navigation correction:** “Find ideas” and “Find a next step” must return to the category's suggestion list. The earlier mock only contained the three already-saved tasks, so both routes appeared exhausted. Both progress previews now include two further three-card batches from the existing sick-day draft for navigation review. A task opened from suggestions returns there; adding with + stays in suggestions and preserves the saved action on My list. “Show other ideas” advances to an unseen batch; explicitly labeled “Revisit earlier ideas” can revisit unselected options. Saved/completed actions are excluded from fresh batches. At real exhaustion, provide clear My list/Explore exits. Runtime specific-request AI remains a separate established capability, not a claim that this mock generates new tasks.

## Progress rules

- Count whole, distinct tasks completed in the category. Saving, reading, generating and substeps do not earn completion progress.
- Keep factual completion state separate from one-time elephant awards. Undo corrects the count without taking away prior playful growth. Re-completing the same canonical action does not award twice or repeat a seen milestone.
- A reached milestone can be replayed deliberately from My list. Replaying changes neither task state nor awards and can recover a missed celebration.
- Completed work is visible in a compact history. Research completion must match its stated outcome; a listing or unanswered inquiry does not prove help is available.
- The bar fills at ten but useful preparation remains available. Do not automatically enroll her in another ten-step block or label her fully safe.
- A reflection can be skipped and does not determine task eligibility. Current needs, explicit constraints and real prerequisites still determine suitable suggestions.
- Keep supportive acknowledgement authored. No AI call for completion, navigation, progress or milestone copy.
- No daily/weekly logging, streak penalties, coins, feeding, new building game, or loss for absence in V0.
