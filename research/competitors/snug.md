# Snug Safety

Checked 2026-09-23. Primary-source facts unless labeled inference.

- **relation:** Closest audience overlap; functional substitute for check-ins, not a holistic planning generator
- **audience:** People living alone, explicitly designed with seniors in mind
- **job:** Daily confirmation that someone is okay, with escalation after a missed check-in
- **platform geography:** iPhone and Android. New accounts: 50 US states, DC, Puerto Rico; US mobile number required. Dispatch wellness checks unavailable abroad.
- **price:** Free daily check-in; up to three check-ins $17.99/year; Dispatch $19.99/month or $149.99/year.
- **features:** Missed check-in SMS and location to contacts; Dispatch adds calls and coordination of a wellness-check request. Local agencies determine response.
- **traction:** Company reports 30m+ cumulative check-ins and 2,400+ five-star reviews; not unique users or paid accounts.
- **gap inference:** A plan generator must help users establish actual arrangements beyond this existing free check-in utility.
