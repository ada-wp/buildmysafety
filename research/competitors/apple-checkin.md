# Apple Check In

Checked 2026-09-23. Primary-source facts unless labeled inference.

- **relation:** Built-in substitute; basic free alternative on compatible owned hardware
- **audience:** iPhone users and their trusted contacts
- **job:** Let someone know arrival or alert when timer/trip does not finish as expected
- **platform geography:** Sender and recipient require iOS 17+. Location sharing unsupported in South Korea and may face other regional limits.
- **price:** Included Messages feature on compatible iPhones; no separate Check In subscription described.
- **features:** Timer or travel-based check-in; selected location, battery/network and optionally route information shared if check-in fails.
- **traction:** No adoption figure used.
- **gap inference:** Discovery and assisted setup are more promising than reimplementing free OS features. A suitable trusted recipient is still required.
