# Life360

Checked 2026-09-23. Primary-source facts unless labeled inference.

- **relation:** Large adjacent family/location safety platform, not a direct women-living-alone planning competitor
- **audience:** Families and trusted Circles
- **job:** Ongoing location coordination and safety services
- **platform geography:** Mobile app; international company. Feature availability varies by membership and region; prices below from US-facing site.
- **price:** Displayed monthly US plans: Free $0; Silver $9.99; Gold $16.99; Platinum $24.99. Annual billing available.
- **features:** Location history and alerts; higher plans add emergency dispatch, roadside assistance and other protection services.
- **traction:** Company Q2 2026 financial release, Aug 10: about 102.4m global MAU, 3.2m paying Circles; $115.6m quarterly subscription revenue ($111.1m core subscription revenue). These are not women-living-alone customers or planning purchases.
- **gap inference:** Demonstrates money spent on recurring operational services. Does not validate subscription demand for advice. Existing trusted-contact network is an advantage.
