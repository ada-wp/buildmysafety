# Noonlight

Checked 2026-09-23. Primary-source facts unless labeled inference.

- **relation:** Adjacent emergency-response competitor and potential recommended resource
- **audience:** Consumers wanting help when they feel unsafe
- **job:** Quickly activate professional emergency response
- **platform geography:** iOS and Android. US coverage confirmed; broader company API coverage is not evidence every consumer feature works internationally.
- **price:** Core Safety Button and emergency response free on both platforms. Paid iOS features exist; current price not disclosed on opened official pricing help page.
- **features:** iOS free includes Timeline and Safety Network; paid extras include Apple Watch, lock-screen widget and automatic crash response. Android includes Safety Button and Timeline.
- **traction:** No paid consumer count verified in this review.
- **gap inference:** Free emergency utility sets a high comparison bar. Proactive preparedness could complement it, but repeating its function is unattractive.
