All 60 concern IDs match the JSON; update the JSON first, then regenerate/refresh the workbook intentionally when the research changes. The 33 records include proposed templates: do not imply they are 33 live external tools. Source IDs and URLs provide traceability; check each resource before production use.

Six research themes include financial vulnerability. The founder later removed Money from app navigation. Retain that research for learning, but use the [five-category app mapping](../design/app/concern-content.json), whose 25 choices map to these research IDs. The choices are an editorial shortlist, not statistically proven “top five” rankings. Life transitions remain contextual.

The market report discusses earlier paid products and scan/map flows. These are historical hypotheses. [Current product](../docs/current-product.md) is the implementation authority. Interviews and a returning-user pilot are still needed to validate demand, helpfulness and willingness to pay.
