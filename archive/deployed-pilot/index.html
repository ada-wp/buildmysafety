The Safety Map

A practical view of the net around your life.

A safety net is not one thing. It is the combination of people you can call, systems you understand, documents you can find, services you can use, money you can access, and actions you have already practiced.

The map does not hand you a score. It shows what is already solid, what is worth strengthening, and one first block you can put in place this week.
