# Sick-day content for founder review

Prepared 12 October 2026 for the connected development app. These are real authored tasks with stable content references, not fictional technical fixtures. They are marked `editorial_review`: the assistant has checked the writing, distinct outcomes, task contract and linked official sources. **Founder approval and professional review have not happened.** Only the explicit development-preview setting may expose them. Public delivery still excludes this review state.

The source of truth is [catalog.mjs](catalog.mjs). Change a task by adding a new immutable version; do not edit a version already delivered to a person. The app must preserve delivered details and keep personal task state in D1, not this file.

Time means the person's active effort, not a promise of a provider reply or a confirmed arrangement. The check-in task is conditional on already knowing someone she wants to contact. It does not assume that person is nearby or available, and it earns completion for sending/recording a request rather than claiming confirmed support. There is no automatic monitoring.

The first batch can have fewer than three when the constraints leave fewer genuine fits. For example, US check-in and pet-care concerns each have two tasks before constraints; a no-spend pet request has the care note only. Country unknown/non-US excludes the two US resource records. Do not broaden to unrelated concerns, repeat actions, promise service coverage or generate content merely to fill three slots.

## External resources checked

- **211 local finder**, US only. The [official finder](https://211.org/about-us/your-local-211) locates the local directory or contact. [About 211](https://211.org/about-us) describes information/referrals for community needs, including food and transport; the [food page](https://211.org/get-help/food-programs-food-benefits) describes food-assistance leads. The finder is not a service booking. A specific check-in program, free help, eligibility or local availability is not established by these pages. The task asks the person to research or ask, rather than claiming an option is confirmed. 211 access is treated as free; referred services may have fees or eligibility limits.
- **Rover pet sitting**, US coverage for this record only. Its [official page](https://www.rover.com/pet-sitting/) describes drop-in sitter search, profile comparison, contact, meet-and-greet and paid booking. The task concerns cats/dogs and only researches a possible sitter. No endorsement, availability promise, background-check guarantee or emergency-care claim is made. Although browsing is free, the record is deliberately classed as paid because the eventual service costs money; explicit no-spend requests exclude it.

Links were checked on 12 October 2026. Provider facts can change; recheck before public release. The service pages inform resource-use instructions, not medical or safety-effectiveness claims. Other tasks are ordinary nonclinical preparation authored from the established product philosophy; they are not presented as scientific interventions.

## Review and verification

Six focused tests cover the explicit review gate, complete task-output contracts, concern relevance/short coverage, resource-country and no-spend eligibility, explicit pet-interest gating, short-effort matching and nonrepetition of shown outcomes. These are mechanical checks, not proof that each task will help a customer.

Founder review should focus on whether each result leaves a usable option, whether the writing feels warm and practical, and whether the small sick-day collection is sufficient for the first audience test. More content can be added after those concrete outcomes are reviewed.
